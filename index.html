Priča o nastanku The origin story

Počelo je kao bilježnica.
Završilo je kao nešto puno više.

It started as a notebook.
It became something far greater.

Svaki vinogradar zna onaj osjećaj. Kasna jesen, mošt vre u tankovima, temperatura skače i pada, a ti pokušavaš u glavi zadržati kada si zadnji put dodao sumpor, kolika je bila gustoća prošlog tjedna i gdje si zapisao podatke od prošle berbe. Najčešće — na papiru koji više ne možeš naći.

Every winemaker knows that feeling. Late autumn, must fermenting in the tanks, temperature rising and falling, and you're trying to remember in your head when you last added sulfur, what the density was last week, and where you wrote down last harvest's data. Usually — on a piece of paper you can no longer find.

Vinar je nastao iz te frustracije. Ideja je bila jednostavna: digitalna bilježnica za male proizvođače. Mjesto gdje možeš zabilježiti fermentaciju, dodavanja sumpora, gustoće i temperature — sve uredno, sve pretraživivo, sve uvijek uz tebe na mobitelu.

Vinar was born from that frustration. The idea was simple: a digital logbook for small producers. A place to log fermentation, sulfur additions, densities and temperatures — all neat, all searchable, always with you on your phone.

Ali onda se nešto dogodilo. Aplikacija je počela rasti. Dodali smo kalkulator za SO₂ i alkohol. Zatim tablice s referentnim vrijednostima. Zatim tržnicu za opremu i grožđe. I onda — AI asistent koji ne samo da zna opću enologiju, nego zna za Grk iz Lumbarde, za Plavac Mali s Pelješca, za dalmatinski kamen i jadransku sol u tlu. Koji može pročitati etiketu proizvoda s fotografije i odmah izračunati dozu za tvoj volumen vina.

But then something happened. The app started to grow. We added an SO₂ and alcohol calculator. Then reference tables. Then a marketplace for equipment and grapes. And then — an AI assistant that doesn't just know general oenology, but knows about Grk from Lumbarda, about Plavac Mali from Pelješac, about Dalmatian limestone and Adriatic salt in the soil. One that can read a product label from a photo and instantly calculate the dose for your wine volume.

Cilj je ostao isti: pomoći malim proizvođačima da prave bolje vino. Samo je put do tog cilja postao — ljepši.

The goal remained the same: help small producers make better wine. The road to that goal just became — more beautiful.

Fermentation, sulfur, notes — all logged per wine, with date and details. Density chart grows as fermentation progresses.

A personal oenologist who reads your data and gives advice based on your wines — not generic answers from the internet.

Napravljeno za Dalmaciju Built for Dalmatia

Aplikacija koja zna razliku između Grka i Pošipa. Koja razumije što znači "trostruka insolacija" na Dingaču.

An app that knows the difference between Grk and Pošip. That understands what "triple insolation" means on Dingač.

Vinar nije generična vinarska aplikacija prevedena na hrvatski. Svaka preporuka, svaki savjet, svaka tablica uzima u obzir mediteransku klimu, dalmatinski krš i sorte koje ovdje rastu tisućama godina. Jer dalmatinsko vino zaslužuje dalmatinski alat.

Vinar is not a generic wine app translated into Croatian. Every recommendation, every piece of advice, every table takes into account the Mediterranean climate, Dalmatian karst, and varieties that have grown here for thousands of years. Because Dalmatian wine deserves a Dalmatian tool.
